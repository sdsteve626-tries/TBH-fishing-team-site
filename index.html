About

Fishing is the hook. The rest is the point.

We're a volunteer led, parent-supported bass fishing team competing on lakes across Texas. But the scoreboard isn't really the mission. Kids who fish learn to read water, read weather, and read patience — skills that don't show up on a report card but change how they show up everywhere else.

We're a registered nonprofit, which means every dollar raised goes directly back to the angler providing educational programs, operations and the mission to provide opportunities for youth anglers. Our goal is to never have an angler sits out a tournament due to lack of funds.
